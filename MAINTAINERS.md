## Responsibilities

Maintainers are expected to:

- review and merge contributions;
- protect project safety/privacy invariants;
- triage bugs and feature requests;
- maintain release quality;
- keep contributor documentation accurate;
- coordinate breaking changes;
- handle private security reports;
- keep releases and roadmap direction coherent.

## Becoming a maintainer

Maintainer access is earned through sustained, high-quality participation rather than a fixed contribution count.

Typical signals include:

- multiple accepted contributions;
- accurate technical review;
- reliable follow-through;
- familiarity with parser/provider/shell boundaries;
- respect for local-first and suggestion-only constraints;
- constructive contributor support.

Governance details are in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).
