# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

TermSense welcomes contributors who communicate professionally, disagree constructively, and help keep technical discussion focused on improving the project.

Examples of positive behavior include:

- giving specific, actionable technical feedback;
- assuming good faith while still challenging ideas rigorously;
- respecting different experience levels;
- keeping reviews about the code, design, or behavior rather than the person;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them;
- helping newcomers understand project constraints.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, discrimination, or targeted insults;
- sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention;
- doxxing or publishing another person's private information;
- sustained disruption, spam, or deliberate derailment;
- personal attacks in issues, pull requests, reviews, or discussions;
- using project spaces to intimidate or abuse contributors.

## Technical disagreement

Strong disagreement is allowed. TermSense is a systems/tooling project, and design tradeoffs can be contentious.

Keep disagreements concrete:

- describe the behavior or risk;
- provide reproducible evidence when possible;
- separate preference from correctness;
- propose alternatives;
- accept maintainer decisions once a direction is settled.

## Scope

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, contribution discussions, and other spaces used to coordinate TermSense development.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove abusive content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors or project operations.

Serious conduct concerns should be raised privately with the repository maintainer rather than escalated through a public argument.

For security vulnerabilities, use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not the conduct process.

## Attribution

This policy is intentionally concise and project-specific. It is informed by common open-source community standards while keeping enforcement language appropriate for a small maintainer-led project.
